# S2.3 — Shift fixed, ink-framed eye graphics

**State:** isolated model candidate; not accepted, not installed in the live game. S2.2 remains open pending its own Chromium and independent-review gates. This step follows the explicit request to merge the S2.2 QA harness and move to the next task.

## Authority

The owner-derived crop [`shift.webp`](../../public/assets/art/shift.webp), its [S2.1 reference contract](shift-fidelity.md), and [S2.3 source comparison](../evidence/shift-s23-eye-trace.svg) govern this correction. The illustration shows **two slanted, white quadrilaterals with broad *stationary black frames*** on a graphite dome. The eyes are graphics, not independent mechanisms. They do not track, blink, rotate, glow through the shell, or have pupils, sockets or protruding lenses. Qwerty's separate moving eyelids **must not** be copied to Shift.

The S2.1 bounding rectangles are approximate, and the crop cannot prove depth or rear geometry. S2.3 therefore alters only the front eye decals and leaves the unverified S2.2 dome depth intact.

## Candidate

Input: `workbench/shift/Shift_S22.glb`. Output: **`workbench/shift/Shift_S23.glb`**, generated by `scripts/shift_eyes_s23.py`.

- The original art's approximate **left** frame is x=105–200, y=137–199; the **right** is x=245–343, y=134–197, in the 378×422 crop. The dome spans x=4–374, y=2–226.
- Projecting those rectangles into the existing S2.2 head bounds moves the left eye inward, lifts both frames clear of the rim and balances their sizes without inventing a symmetric design.
- Reproject **the same triangles**, including their white interiors, against the eleven-ring S2.2 dome. The white graphics retain the original border margins instead of filling the outer black rectangles. Thin depth offsets keep both overlays visually in front of the dome, without spherical eye volume.
- Recalculate face normals. **Only 3,456 decal vertices** are changed: 1,728 black-frame vertices and 1,728 white graphic vertices. Preserve the other materials, the outer dome, the torso, skin weights/indices, UV coordinates, 16-bone armature, three clips, and all original animations. No new bones, keys, eyelids, gaze control or weapon changes.
